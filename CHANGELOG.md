**Hotfix Vers. v2.0.0 — BFM Guns-Only Training & Kneeboard Update**

* Added **FREE TRAINING - BFM GUNS** to the F10 menu.
* Available opponents: MiG-29S, Su-27, Su-30, MiG-31, F-15C, F-16C, F/A-18C, JF-17 and J-11A.
* Select individual aircraft types as singles or pairs, or generate random formations of 1, 2 or 4 aircraft.
* All BFM opponents use **Excellent / ACE** skill, full cannon ammunition and no missiles.
* Bandits spawn approximately **5 NM ahead, at the requesting player’s altitude**, inside BFM ZONE.
* AI remains **WEAPONS HOLD through the neutral merge**. Cannon engagement is enabled after a confirmed close pass and separation, accompanied by a **GUNS HOT** message.
* Merge detection follows the requesting player; unrelated players cannot trigger weapons release.
* Added **English kneeboard page 20** covering BFM access, opponents, spawn geometry, merge rules and multiplayer range controls.

**Multiplayer:** BFM shares the existing air-training range. Starting a BFM exercise replaces current scripted air opponents and cancels the active campaign exercise.

**Validation:** Local script checks and mission archive integrity checks passed. In-simulator AI behaviour still requires flight testing.
